FAQs & Help Academy

How does SmartBuyHub track my earnings?
SmartBuyHub uses advanced affiliate tag tracking. When you generate links using our tools and share them, the retailer tracks purchases made via those links and reports commissions back to us, which are added directly to your profile.
When will my pending profit be confirmed?
Profits typically appear as pending within 72 hours of an order being placed. It remains pending during the retailer's return/cancellation policy window (usually 30 days) and gets confirmed once the validation completes successfully.
What is the minimum withdrawal payout limit?
To make withdrawing simple, we have kept our minimum payout limit to just ₹10.00! Once your confirmed profit is ₹10.00 or higher, you can easily request payouts using UPI or Bank Transfers.
How do UPI vs Bank Transfer withdrawals work?
UPI transfers are processed instantly or within 24 hours of approval. Bank transfers undergo bank verification clearances and typically settle in your account within 1-3 business days.
